\subsection{ASR Benchmark}
We evaluate [N] ASR systems (\S\ref{sec:methodology}) on our held-out test
set (\S\ref{sec:data}). Following \citet{tone-yoruba-asr}'s finding that
tone marking materially affects reported Yor\`ub\'a ASR quality, we report
both \emph{tone-sensitive} (Unicode-NFC-normalized, diacritics scored as
real characters) and \emph{tone-agnostic} (tone marks stripped, underdot
letters --- \emph{\d{e} \d{o} \d{s}} --- preserved, since these mark distinct
phonemes rather than tone) WER and CER for every system, computed with the
same scorer (\texttt{phase2-asr/scripts/normalize.py}) across all systems
so no comparison is confounded by inconsistent normalization.
