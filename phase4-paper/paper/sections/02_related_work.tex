\paragraph{Yor\`ub\'a ASR.} \citet{ncair-yoruba-asr} released a Whisper-small
fine-tune for Yor\`ub\'a (NCAIR1/Yoruba-ASR), developed by Awarri
Technologies with Nigeria's Federal Ministry of Communications, Innovation
and Digital Economy. \citet{owe-voice} evaluate monolingual and
multilingual ASR systems on a Yor\`ub\'a proverb speech dataset, finding
that current systems fail to capture cultural/idiomatic nuance.
\citet{tone-yoruba-asr} systematically study the effect of tone marking on
Yor\`ub\'a ASR evaluation, motivating our tone-sensitive/tone-agnostic
scoring split (\S\ref{sec:experiments}). \citet{voices-unheard-yoruba} study
NLP resource gaps across Yor\`ub\'a regional dialects. \citet{african-asr-survey}
provide a systematic review of ASR for African low-resource languages more
broadly, which we build on for related-work framing.

\paragraph{Multilingual and low-resource ASR foundation models.} Meta's
Massive Multilingual Speech (MMS) project \citep{mms} extended wav2vec~2.0
with per-language adapters across 1,000+ languages. More recently,
Omnilingual ASR \citep{omnilingual2025} extends coverage to 1,600+ languages
(5,400+ via zero-shot in-context learning) with an Apache~2.0 license,
built in part with Mozilla Common Voice, African Next Voices, and Lanfrica.
To our knowledge, no prior work benchmarks Omnilingual ASR specifically on
Yor\`ub\'a against the existing Yor\`ub\'a-specific baselines above.

\paragraph{Yor\`ub\'a speech data.} \citet{naijavoices} release the
NaijaVoices dataset, $\sim$1,800 hours of speech across Igbo, Hausa, and
Yor\`ub\'a from 5,000+ speakers (CC~BY-NC-SA~4.0). \citet{iroyinspeech}
release \`Ir\`oy\`inSpeech, a $\sim$48-hour Yor\`ub\'a speech corpus spanning
news and creative-writing domains. We use [DATASETS — fill in from Phase 1]
for training and evaluation.

\paragraph{Text-to-speech for Nigerian languages.} YarnGPT2
\citep{yarngpt2} is an open TTS model supporting Yor\`ub\'a, Igbo, Hausa,
and Nigerian-accented English via pure language modeling (no external
phonetic adapters), which we use for the speech-output stage of our
pipeline (\S\ref{sec:methodology}).

\paragraph{Positioning.} Prior work evaluates Yor\`ub\'a ASR
(\citealp{ncair-yoruba-asr,owe-voice,tone-yoruba-asr}) and Yor\`ub\'a/Nigerian
TTS (\citealp{yarngpt2}) as independent components. We are, to our
knowledge, the first to evaluate a full speech-in, speech-out conversational
system with multi-turn dialogue state for Yor\`ub\'a, and the first to
benchmark Omnilingual ASR against existing Yor\`ub\'a baselines under
tone-aware scoring.
